## Overview

Botverio is a founder-led software product and business website for selling ready-made Telegram bots and offering custom bot development services. The platform presents bot categories, service positioning, bot details, testimonials, content pages, and contact flows for customer requests.

The project shows full ownership of a live product: product framing, frontend implementation, backend content modeling, deployment setup, and the practical decisions needed to turn a technical service into a public website.

## Problem

Many small businesses want automation through Telegram bots, but they often need a clearer way to understand available bot types, compare use cases, and request custom development. A simple portfolio page is not enough for this type of service because the site must explain the offer, capture leads, and support future content growth.

## Solution

Botverio organizes the service around a public website and CMS-backed product structure. The frontend presents the brand, bot catalog, bot detail pages, custom development services, and contact paths. The backend supports structured content for bots, categories, testimonials, leads, and blog-style content.

## Technical Highlights

- React frontend for a responsive product and service website.
- Strapi backend for CMS-driven bot, category, testimonial, lead, and content management.
- PostgreSQL-oriented data model for production-ready structured content.
- REST API integration between frontend pages and backend content.
- VPS deployment on Hetzner infrastructure with Nginx and HTTPS setup.
- Release-oriented server structure with production assets and operational configuration.
- Product copy and information architecture designed around customer discovery and lead generation.

## Future Improvements

- Add richer bot comparison pages and use-case filters.
- Add analytics around page visits, contact intent, and conversion paths.
- Expand the content section with automation guides and Telegram bot case studies.
- Add an internal admin workflow for tracking leads and custom development requests.
